# μ(sic)

> **We are no longer building software that remembers your music.**
>
> **We are building software that helps people discover the hidden connections behind the music they love.**
>
> *(我們不再打造一個記住你聽過什麼的軟體；我們要打造的是一個幫助人們發現他們所愛音樂背後隱藏連結的產品。)*

---

This repository contains the codebase for **μ(sic)**, a platform designed to help people understand themselves through their listening behavior.

## Archived Product Direction {已歸檔的產品探索方向}

Version 0.1 explored the idea of a Music Journal.

After real-world evaluation, we decided not to continue this direction.

The prototype remains archived as an important milestone in the evolution of μ(sic).

*(版本 0.1 探索了「音樂日記」的概念。經過實際的真人測試後，我們決定不再繼續這個方向。此原型將被保留，作為 μ(sic) 演化過程中的重要里程碑。)*
