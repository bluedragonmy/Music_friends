# Mission VII: Learn Why We're Wrong

This document tracks user testing sessions, qualitative feedback protocols, and invalidated hypotheses. Our goal is not to prove that the product is great, but to discover exactly where our assumptions were wrong.

---

## 1. Beta Testing Protocol {封測與質性研究協定}

### 5-Person Silent Observation {五人靜默觀察}
- **Scale**: Exactly 5 users.
- **Protocol**: Sit next to them, record screen/actions, and remain completely silent. Do not guide.
- **Measurements**: Timestamps of pauses, scrolls, navigation, and leaving.
- **One Question**: At the end of the session, ask only: **「你還記得剛剛哪一句話嗎？」** (Do you remember what sentence you just read?). If they remember it 5 minutes later, the copy had weight. If they forgot, it was just pretty words.
- **Success Criteria**: A first-time user pauses at a sentence and looks silently for at least 10 seconds.

---

## 2. Core Metrics {關鍵質性指標}

- **Return With Intention {意圖留存}**: The user closes μ(sic), but voluntarily opens it later due to recalling a phrase or wondering what today's music story says, without push notification prompts.
- **Stay Time Telemetry**: Monitored silently on dashboard cards to verify deep engagement (Target > 15s avg stay time).

## 3. Things We Thought But Were Wrong {被推翻的假設紀錄}

Every user research cycle must add at least one entry here. If no assumptions are invalidated, we haven't learned anything.

- ❌ **Assumption**: Users need technical metadata labels (like "Observation" or "Today's Note") to navigate.
  - *Reality*: Stripping labels and displaying only dates makes the interface cleaner and more intimate.
  - *Date Invalidated*: 2026-07-05
- ❌ **Assumption**: We need 30+ Google Forms responses to evaluate Beta value.
  - *Reality*: Recording 5 real users screen-by-screen while sitting in silence reveals 10x more design friction than self-reported feedback forms.
  - *Date Invalidated*: 2026-07-05
- ❌ **Assumption**: Poetic Era titles (like "The Season of Gentle Flows") help users relate to their music patterns.
  - *Reality*: Users feel they are "fake" and engineered, preferring clean, physical time stamps.
  - *Date Invalidated*: 2026-07-05
- ❌ **Assumption**: Connecting users who listen to the same song via anonymous chatrooms enhances reflection.
  - *Reality*: It shifts the product focus from "relationship with yourself" to social networking, diluting the core value.
  - *Date Invalidated*: 2026-07-05
